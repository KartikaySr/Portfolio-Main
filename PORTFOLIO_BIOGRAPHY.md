#### Overview

GridForge-AI is an edge-first energy-intelligence simulation that makes operational recommendations traceable, bounded, human-authorized, and verifiable.

#### Technology

React, TypeScript, Tauri, Rust, Python, FastAPI, SQLite, and PostgreSQL.

#### Work and implementation

- Designed a **14-stage** decision and evidence flow covering telemetry validation, human authorization, simulated dispatch, verification, and audit.

- Explicit unknown and degraded states.

- Simulation-only factory inputs and responses.

#### Architecture

1. **Telemetry:** Synthetic inputs with quality checks.

3. **Rust supervisor:** Lifecycle management and bounded IPC.
4. **FastAPI runtime:** Recommendation and evidence flow.
5. **SQLite · PostgreSQL:** Outbox, synchronization, and audit records.

#### Evidence and metrics
